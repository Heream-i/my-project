Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you visit our website and subscribe to our newsletter.

In short: We only collect your email address if you voluntarily provide it to receive our essays. We will never sell, rent, or trade your data with third parties for marketing purposes.

1. Information We Collect

We keep data collection minimal. The only personal information we collect directly is:

  • Identity & Contact Data: Your email address, which you provide voluntarily when you subscribe via our newsletter sign-up form.

We may also automatically collect basic technical data via standard server logs, such as anonymised IP addresses, browser types, and your time spent on the page, to monitor site performance.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your collected email address strictly to:

  • Deliver our long-form essays, commentaries, and platform updates directly to your inbox.
  • Respond to inquiries if you contact us directly.

The legal basis for processing this data is your explicit consent, given when you type your email into our form and click "Subscribe".

3. Data Retention and Third Parties

We retain your email address for as long as your subscription remains active. If you unsubscribe, your details are removed from our active mailing directory.

To safely manage our distribution list, we use trusted, third-party email delivery services (such as Substack, Mailchimp, or ConvertKit). These platforms act as data processors and are contractually prohibited from using your data for any purpose other than executing our newsletter mailings.

4. Cookies

This website uses essential cookies required for basic functionality and site stability. We do not use intrusive third-party tracking, advertising cookies, or behavioral targeting scripts.

5. Your Legal Rights

Under applicable data protection laws (including the UK GDPR), you have rights regarding your personal information, including:

  • The right to withdraw consent: You can cancel your subscription at any time by clicking the "Unsubscribe" link located at the bottom of every newsletter email.
  • The right to access or erase: You may request a copy of the email data we hold, or request its immediate deletion, by contacting the site administrator.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please contact us through our website's designated contact channels.
